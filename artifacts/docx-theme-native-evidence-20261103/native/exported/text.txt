Cooling experiment report
Comparison of insulated and uninsulated vessels
We compare how two vessels cool from 80 degrees C in a room held at 20 degrees C. The insulated
vessel cools more slowly. The chart and measurements below describe the same cooling model.



Experimental setup
Each vessel contains the same water volume. A probe records temperature every five minutes. Keep
the lid position and room conditions unchanged.
   1. Record the initial temperature before starting the timer.
   2. Keep both probes at the same depth.
   3. Read the measurements without moving either vessel.
